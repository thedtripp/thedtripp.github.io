Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

DD Tripp is a software company based in San Francisco, California. This policy describes what information DD Tripp collects through ddtripp.com, its software services, and its developer tools, and how that information is used. Questions or requests: info@ddtripp.com.

Information collected

Website. ddtripp.com is a static informational website. It does not run advertising trackers and does not sell personal information. Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps) may be recorded by the hosting provider and used only for security and reliability.

DD Tripp Analytics (LinkedIn tool). DD Tripp Analytics is an internal tool used to measure the performance of DD Tripp's own LinkedIn posts. With authorization through LinkedIn's official OAuth flow, the tool retrieves post performance metrics (such as impressions, reach, reactions, comments, reshares, saves, and link clicks) for DD Tripp's own posts and LinkedIn Page via LinkedIn's official APIs. This data is stored on DD Tripp's own systems, is used only to evaluate DD Tripp's own content, and is not shared with, sold to, or used on behalf of any third party. Access can be revoked at any time from LinkedIn's settings, and stored metrics are deleted on request to the email above.

Software services and apps. DD Tripp's mobile apps are designed to work offline and do not collect personal information. If you contact DD Tripp by email, your email address and message are used only to respond. If paid services are offered in the future, account and billing information will be collected only as needed to provide the service, and this policy will be updated first.

Sharing

DD Tripp does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information is disclosed only when required by law.

Retention and your choices

Information is kept only as long as it serves the purposes above. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at info@ddtripp.com.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
