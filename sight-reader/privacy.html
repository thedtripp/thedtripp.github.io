Sight Reader Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Summary

Sight Reader does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app works entirely offline.

What stays on your device

Your settings and practice history are saved only on your device so the app can show your progress. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we don't do

Sight Reader has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services. It does not use the internet and does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or other device data.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on Sight Reader's App Store or Google Play listing.
